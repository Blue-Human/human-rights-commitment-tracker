# HRCT AI-assisted research pilot — Spain UPR fourth cycle

Date: 2 October 2026

Purpose: test whether part of the HRCT evidence-gathering and assessment workflow can be accelerated with AI while preserving human review as the final methodological control.

## Pilot records

### 50.11 — Adequate means for OBERAXE

**Pilot finding:** Insufficient evidence (`unable_to_assess`)  
**Confidence:** Medium

Official information shows meaningful operational capacity in online hate-speech monitoring: a dedicated eight-person monitoring team, daily monitoring throughout the year, dedicated technical systems (ALERTODIO and FARO), five major social platforms and regular monitoring outputs. The reviewed public evidence does not disclose OBERAXE's total budget, total staffing or workload across its full statutory mandate, nor a benchmark for adequate resourcing. The evidence therefore supports activity and capacity but not a reliable conclusion on adequacy.

Primary source: https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/oberaxe/monitorizacion-discurso-de-odio-ilegal-en-linea

### 50.17 — Strategic Framework against racism and xenophobia

**Pilot finding:** Substantial progress (`substantially_implemented`)  
**Confidence:** Medium

OBERAXE and the Permanent Observatory of Immigration published a monitoring panel for the 2023-2027 Strategic Framework covering 6 policy blocks, 23 lines of action and 45 tactical objectives. OBERAXE also continues operational monitoring and publication activity through 2026. These are concrete implementation and accountability mechanisms. The evidence reviewed does not establish full and effective implementation of every policy line or provide an independent outcome evaluation of the framework as a whole.

Primary sources:
- https://www.inclusion.gob.es/en/web/oberaxe/w/panel-de-indicadores-de-seguimiento-del-marco-estrategico-de-ciudadania-e-inclusion-contra-el-racismo-y-la-xenofobia
- https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/oberaxe/monitorizacion-discurso-de-odio-ilegal-en-linea

### 50.19 — Third Action Plan to Combat Hate Crimes

**Pilot finding:** Implemented (`implemented`)  
**Confidence:** High

The Ministry of the Interior published the III Action Plan to Combat Hate Crimes 2025-2028 in 2025. In June 2026 the Ministry reported the second meeting of the Plan's Monitoring Commission. On the narrow wording of the UPR recommendation — advance with adoption of the third plan — adoption has occurred and the plan is operational. This finding does not imply that the plan has reduced hate crime or that every measure is effective.

Primary sources:
- https://www.interior.gob.es/opencms/pdf/archivos-y-documentacion/documentacion-y-publicaciones/publicaciones-descargables/seguridad-ciudadana/3_plan-accion-delitos-odio_126250364_pdfWEB.pdf
- https://www.interior.gob.es/opencms/gl/detalle/articulo/Los-delitos-e-incidentes-de-odio-aumentaron-un-236-por-ciento-en-2025-y-registraron-la-mayor-cifra-de-la-serie-historica/

### 50.21 — Second National Human Rights Plan

**Pilot finding:** Limited progress (`limited_progress`)  
**Confidence:** Medium

Spain has a formal monitoring and participation architecture for the II National Human Rights Plan. Royal Decree 709/2024 gives civil-society organisations, councils, universities and experts roles in the Steering Committee and establishes monitoring responsibilities. A June 2026 BOE resolution documents a concrete cooperation agreement with the Asociación Pro Derechos Humanos de España for activities within the Plan. These facts show continued implementation and participation, but the reviewed public evidence does not provide a comprehensive current evaluation of execution or effectiveness across the Plan's 421 measures.

Primary sources:
- https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2024/07/23/709
- https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-13715

## Methodological note

These assessments were generated as an AI-assisted pilot from public sources and written into the HRCT dataset with evidence links and append-only assessment history. They are explicitly marked in the assessment rationale as requiring human validation before external launch. The pilot is intended to test research acceleration, not to replace peer review.
